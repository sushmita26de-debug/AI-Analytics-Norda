Measures

Total revenue
Sum of revenue. Per line: quantity × unit_price × (1 − discount_rate), rounded half-up to the cent.
Orders
Distinct count of order_id. Orders, not lines.
Units sold
Sum of quantity.
Discount amount
Sum of discount_amount = quantity × unit_price × discount_rate.
Stock on hand
Sum of qty_on_hand at the latest month in the selection. Never summed across months.

Definitions

Best-selling products
Ranked by revenue.
Ran out
qty_on_hand = 0 at month end.
Countries
All shown by default. Use is_core_market to filter to the core markets.
Stock date
Inventory is keyed to the month-end date, so one month filter works on both facts.

Limits

Stock vs. country and channel
The inventory data has no country or channel, so these filters don't change stock on hand.
Returns
Revenue is before returns. Returns are handled in a separate system.
Cost of running out
Needs a lost-sales estimate, which isn't in the data yet.
